**One breath, one movement.**
Start in extended child's pose, hips on the heels. Inhale — gaze to the navel, cat, shift forward into tabletop. Exhale — lift the head, cow, back to child's pose. Repeat three times.
Key points:
- Let the breath move the body, not the other way round — the movement is as long as the breath.
- In cat, round the spine vertebra by vertebra from the tailbone, navel draws in.
- In cow, open the chest forward, shoulders away from the ears, don't crunch the neck.
- Hands keep pressing; feel how the load on the wrists changes as you shift.
