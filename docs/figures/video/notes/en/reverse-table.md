**Open the front of the body.**
Slowly lower the leg, both feet on the floor, hands press behind you, lift the body — reverse tabletop, two breaths.
Key points:
- Fingers point toward the feet, shoulders over the wrists.
- Feet hip-width, knees over the ankles, thighs parallel to the floor.
- Push the hips up and open the chest — the front body becomes a flat table.
- The head may relax back; if the neck complains, look forward with the chin slightly tucked.
