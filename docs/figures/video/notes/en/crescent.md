**Front foot and back instep press together.**
Inhale, hands beside the front foot, back foot one step back, top of the back foot lowers. If stable, lift the upper body, arms up — crescent lunge, three breaths.
Key points:
- Front shin vertical, knee over the ankle.
- Front sole and back instep press at the same time — this takes load off the back knee.
- Pelvis sinks, tailbone down; feel the stretch in the hip flexor and groin of the back leg.
- Ribs don't flare, arms reach up, shoulders relaxed.
Easier: blanket under the back knee; hands on the front knee.
