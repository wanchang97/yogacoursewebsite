**Rest after inversions.**
Sit the hips back on the heels, forehead on the mat, arms beside the body or reaching forward; stay a few breaths.
Key points:
- After inversions stay at least 3–5 breaths so blood pressure and breath settle.
- Forehead down, neck fully relaxed.
- Knees may open so the belly rests between the thighs.
- Whenever you feel tired, come back to child's pose — rest is part of practice.
