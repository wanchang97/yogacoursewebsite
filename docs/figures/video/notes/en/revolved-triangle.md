**One hand presses firmly, the other reaches up.**
Press one hand firmly onto a block or the floor, stretch the other arm up, gaze to the top fingers — revolved triangle. Let the breath guide you and open chest and belly toward the top hand.
Key points:
- Lengthen the spine first, then twist — twisting with a rounded back strains the lower back.
- A block under the lower hand is a good choice; a flat back matters more.
- Keep the pelvis as level as possible; the rotation happens in the thoracic spine.
- Each inhale lengthen, each exhale turn a little more.
