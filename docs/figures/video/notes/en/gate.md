**Gate pose: open the groin, lengthen the side waist.**
Place one foot out to the side, kneel upright and stretch the groin. Arms up, bend toward the extended leg three times, stretching the opposite waist; hold the last one for two breaths.
Key points:
- The kneeling knee is under the hip; the extended foot is roughly in line with it.
- Lengthen up first, then bend; the chest faces forward and doesn't collapse.
- The pelvis stays steady — the stretch is in the upper side waist and between the ribs.
- If the kneeling knee is uncomfortable, put a blanket under it.
