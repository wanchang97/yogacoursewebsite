**Steady base, open upper body.**
Inhale, turn the back foot 90 degrees, open the side of the body, gaze over the front hand — Warrior II, five breaths.
Key points:
- Front shin vertical, knee directly over the ankle, not falling in.
- The front foot and the outer edge of the back foot press evenly; the pelvis sinks and roots.
- Arms reach out to the sides — long but not tense; shoulders sink.
- The body grows up from the midline; the torso stays centred between the legs, not leaning over the front leg.
- Gaze beyond the front fingertips — find calm and width.
