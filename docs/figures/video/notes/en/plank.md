**Plank: lengthen yourself from both ends.**
Exhale, step the feet back one after the other into plank. Feet hip-width, shoulders over the wrists.
Key points:
- Tailbone lengthens toward the heels while the pubic bone draws gently toward the head — like pulling a drawer open from both ends.
- Belly slightly engaged; one straight line from head to heels — no sagging, no hips up.
- Fingers spread and grip the floor; push away so the upper back stays full, shoulder blades don't collapse.
- The neck continues the spine; gaze just in front of the hands.
Easier: kneeling plank — knees down, same straight line.
