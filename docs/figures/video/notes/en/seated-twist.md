**First lengthen, then twist.**
Inhale, arms up, make the body long. Exhale, twist to one side: the opposite hand on the knee, the other hand behind the back resting at the waist. The upper body stays calm, the gaze slowly turns. 1–2 breaths.
Key points:
- Inhale to grow taller; exhale to turn a little further at that height.
- The twist starts in the belly, then chest and shoulders, and only last the head and gaze.
- Both sit bones stay evenly grounded.
- The hand on the knee only assists — don't lever the body with the arm.
