**Half pigeon → full pigeon → forward fold.**
Inhale, place the front leg across the mat parallel to the short edge, adjust the back leg — half pigeon, five deep breaths. Front leg stays; slowly bring the back leg to the side — full pigeon. Inhale, arms up; exhale, fold down, keep space in front, gaze forward; once belly and chest come closer to the leg, lower the gaze. Relax for five breaths.
Key points:
- The angle of the front shin sets the intensity — the closer the foot to the body, the easier.
- Flex the front foot to protect the knee; if the knee feels pressure, bring the foot closer or put a blanket under the hip.
- Keep the pelvis as level as possible; if the front-leg hip floats, put a block under it.
- The back instep reaches back, knee facing down.
- Hands press back into down dog, step one leg forward, prepare the other side.
