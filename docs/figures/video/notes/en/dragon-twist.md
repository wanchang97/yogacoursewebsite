**Hand pushes the knee, chest opens up.**
Lower the back knee, turn one forearm in 90° on the mat, stretch the other arm up, or let that hand push the front knee gently out, two breaths.
Key points:
- Make sure the back knee is comfortable (blanket), then twist.
- Pushing the knee out opens the hip and lets the chest turn toward the ceiling.
- The supporting forearm presses firmly, the shoulder doesn't collapse.
- Gaze follows the top hand as far as the neck is comfortable.
