**Preparing for tiger: send the leg far back.**
From tabletop, step one foot back, straighten this leg, toes on the floor, and gently rock forward and back.
Key points:
- The back leg reaches from the hip, heel pushes back — feel the back of the leg.
- Rocking forward and back moves the shoulders over the wrists and warms them up.
- Pelvis stays level and facing down; the hip of the extended leg doesn't open.
- This builds the hand–core–leg connection for tiger.
