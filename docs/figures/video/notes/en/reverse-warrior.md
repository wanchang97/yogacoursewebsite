**Legs stay, the upper body bends back.**
Inhale, keep the legs in Warrior II, the front hand reaches up and back, the back hand slides lightly onto the back leg — reverse warrior. Then return to Warrior II.
Key points:
- The front knee stays over the ankle; don't let it retreat as you lean back.
- You open the front side waist: lengthen up first, then bend back.
- The back hand only rests on the leg, no weight on it.
- Gaze may go up to the fingertips; if the neck complains, look forward.
