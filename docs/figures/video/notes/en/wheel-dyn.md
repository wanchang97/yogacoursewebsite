**Press up step by step.**
Lie on your back, knees bent, feet hip-width and close to the buttocks. Hands beside the ears, fingers pointing to the shoulders. Inhale, lift the hips first, then place the crown of the head lightly on the mat, finally press the hands, lift the head and straighten the arms.
Key points:
- Enter in stages: hips → crown on the floor → arms straight; steady each stage first.
- Elbows point back, not out; knees parallel with the feet, not splaying.
- The crown on the floor is only a brief transition — don't load head and neck for long.
- If wrists or shoulders aren't ready, stay in bridge.
