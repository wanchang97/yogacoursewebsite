**Open the hip and the front of the thigh together.**
In dragon with the back knee down, bend the knee to lift the back foot and reach back with one hand to hold the top of the foot, gently drawing the heel toward the buttock.
Key points:
- Make sure the supporting hand and front leg are steady before reaching back.
- Once you hold the foot, don't yank — let the front of the thigh open with the breath.
- Pelvis forward and down, chest opens forward.
- If you can't reach the foot, use a strap or simply bend the knee.
- Pad the back knee with a blanket; come out if the knee hurts.
