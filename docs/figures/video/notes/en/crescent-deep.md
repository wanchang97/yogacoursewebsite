**Deepen the backbend from stability.**
In crescent, if the base is steady, let the pelvis sink further while the chest opens up and back, arms leading the body backward.
Key points:
- Depth comes from "hips down + chest up", not from folding the lower back.
- Keep the belly gently engaged to protect the lower back.
- The head follows the chest — don't throw it back first; if the neck complains, look forward.
- Only go deeper while the breath stays steady; if it shortens, return to regular crescent.
