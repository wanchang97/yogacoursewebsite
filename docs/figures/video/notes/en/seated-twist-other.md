**Other side, same order: grow tall, then turn from the belly.**
Inhale, arms up, lengthen. Exhale, twist to the other side, hand to the opposite knee, the other hand behind you at the waist, gaze slowly follows. 1–2 breaths.
Key points:
- Notice the difference between the sides — no need to make them equal, just observe.
- Pause briefly at the end of each exhale and feel the belly draw in.
- Come back slowly too: gaze first, then shoulders, chest, belly.
