**Belly to thigh first.**
Turn to face the short edge of the mat. Inhale, arms forward and up. Exhale, fold forward; hands on blocks beside you or on the floor — pyramid.
Key points:
- Both hips face forward; back foot about 45°, heel grounded.
- Fold from the hips with a long back; don't force the head to the leg.
- Order: belly finds the thigh, then the chest comes closer to the leg, and finally the face sinks toward the knee by itself.
- Front knee soft to protect the back of the leg.
Advanced: when stable, inhale to lengthen further, exhale and try interlacing the fingers behind the back.
