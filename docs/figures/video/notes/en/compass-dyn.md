**Shin and forehead move toward each other.**
Inhale, hold the heel with both hands, slowly straighten the knee, upper body upright. Bring shin and forehead toward each other three times.
Key points:
- The shin comes to the forehead — don't drop the head to the shin; keep the back upright.
- Move slowly and feel hip and hamstrings waking up.
- If the shin is still far from the forehead, stay at this step and skip the full compass.
