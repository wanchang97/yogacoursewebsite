**The starting point of every standing pose.**
Place heel, the ball under the little toe and the ball under the big toe one after the other — build a stable stand. Activate the inner, outer and back of the legs. Pelvis level, spine upright, shoulders sink down and back, arms by your sides. Take three deep breaths.
Key points:
- Imagine three points under each foot rooting like a tripod, weight evenly spread.
- Knees not locked, thighs slightly rotating in, tailbone naturally down.
- The crown reaches up, chin parallel to the floor.
- Once grounded, bring attention back to the breath — the stillness before the flow.
