**While holding, let go of effort.**
Stay in the fold for a few deep breaths; you may hold the foot of the straight leg.
Key points:
- Feel the hip of the lotus leg slowly opening with each exhale.
- Back long: belly to thigh, then chest, then forehead.
- Advanced: the hand on the lotus side reaches behind the back to hold the lotus foot (half bind) — only if the knee is completely comfortable.
- Any sharp sensation in the knee: come out immediately and do head-to-knee instead.
