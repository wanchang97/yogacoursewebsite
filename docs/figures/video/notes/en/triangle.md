**A little higher with each inhale, a little lower with each exhale.**
Slowly straighten the front leg and shorten the stance a little. Inhale, arms wide. Exhale, shift the upper body over the front leg. At your limit: inhale, hands together in front of the chest; exhale, one arm reaches up, the other down, gaze to the top hand — triangle.
Key points:
- The lower hand can rest on the shin, a block or the foot — choose where the chest can still open.
- Each inhale the top arm reaches higher; each exhale the lower hand reaches lower.
- Once stable, follow the gaze upward and keep opening chest and belly; the chest turns toward the ceiling.
- Both feet firmly grounded, front knee very slightly bent — don't hyperextend.
