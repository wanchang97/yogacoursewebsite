**From plow, send the legs to the sky.**
From plow, support the back with both hands (as close to the shoulder blades as possible); inhale, lift one leg, then the other, until the body is vertical.
Key points:
- Elbows shoulder-width and drawn in, helping the upper back lift.
- Lifting the legs is "pushing up", not falling toward the head.
- Head and neck stay still; gaze toward the chest or toes.
- If unsteady, lift just one leg or stay in plow.
