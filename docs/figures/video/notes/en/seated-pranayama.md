**A stable seat is the basis for breathing.**
You can sit on a yoga block so that your knees are lower than your hips and rest close to the mat. A stable body brings relaxed muscles and a calm mind.
Common seated positions:
- 🧘 **Full lotus**: each foot rests on the opposite thigh, close to the hip crease.
- **Half lotus**: one foot on the opposite thigh, the other leg on the mat.
- **Easy pose**: both legs rest on the mat.
- **Double pigeon** (fire log, Agnistambhasana): shins stacked, each foot above the opposite knee.
Choose whatever lets you sit most stable and comfortable right now — once you are settled, begin to observe your breath.
