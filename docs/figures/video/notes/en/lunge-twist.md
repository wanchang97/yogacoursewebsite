**A twist in the crescent lunge.**
Hands together overhead, down in front of the chest, then turn toward the front-leg side, two breaths.
Key points:
- Inhale to grow tall, exhale to twist — starting from the belly, then chest, then gaze.
- You can hook the opposite elbow outside the front knee and use the resistance to deepen the twist.
- The front knee doesn't fall in; it stays over the ankle.
- Back knee down or lifted — stability first.
