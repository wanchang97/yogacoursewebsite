**Arm around the knee, hands bind behind the back.**
Challenge: stretch the opposite arm outside the bent knee, or wrap it around the outer thigh and clasp the hands behind the back — Marichyasana, three breaths.
Key points:
- First make sure the spine is upright in the twist, then wrap; if the hands don't meet, use a strap or hold the wrist.
- Once bound, don't collapse the chest — keep turning up and back.
- Both sit bones stay grounded as much as possible.
