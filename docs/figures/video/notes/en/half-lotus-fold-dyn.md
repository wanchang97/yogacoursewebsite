**Knee safety comes first.**
Place one foot at the top of the opposite thigh (half lotus), other leg straight. Inhale, arms up; exhale, fold forward. Move back and forth with the breath a few times.
Key points:
- Half lotus comes from external rotation of the hip, not from twisting the knee — if the foot doesn't reach the hip crease, rest it lower on the thigh or switch to head-to-knee.
- The lotus foot stays relaxed; don't overbend the ankle.
- As you fold, the lotus knee may drift toward the floor — never push it down with the hand.
- Lengthen on the inhale, move forward on the exhale, no rush to the bottom.
