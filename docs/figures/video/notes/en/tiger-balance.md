**Tiger balance variations.**
While holding you can deepen: ① extend the opposite arm and leg into one line; ② further: the opposite hand reaches back for the ankle — hold tiger steadily.
Key points:
- Stage one: one line from fingertips to toes, reaching in both directions.
- When holding the ankle, kick the foot into the hand so the chest opens forward — don't pull the foot in.
- Supporting hand and knee press firmly; keep the pelvis as level as possible.
- If unsteady, stay in stage one — balance is also concentration practice.
