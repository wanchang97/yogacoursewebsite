**One hand holds the knee, the other side lengthens.**
Keep hugging the knee; exhale, slowly extend one arm and turn the gaze to its fingertips or the floor, stretching that side, two breaths. Inhale, turn back to center.
Key points:
- The extended arm is at shoulder height, reaching far.
- Turn the gaze slowly; if balance wobbles, look at the floor.
- The standing hip doesn't push out; pelvis stays level.
