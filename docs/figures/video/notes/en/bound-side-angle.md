**Hands bind behind the back.**
If stable, thread the lower arm under the front leg, wrap the top arm behind the back and clasp the hands — bound side angle. Then return to Warrior II.
Key points:
- Make sure the base is stable before binding; if the hands don't meet, hold a strap.
- The bind helps open the shoulder — keep the chest turning up instead of collapsing to reach.
- If the breath gets short, release and go back one step.
