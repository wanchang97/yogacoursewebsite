**Notice where it pulls most.**
Slowly take hold of the outside of the foot of the straight leg and stay for five deep breaths.
Key points:
- Feel where the tension is: the back of the straight leg, or the calf or hip of the bent leg.
- Back long: belly to thigh first, forehead to knee last.
- If you can't reach the foot, hold the shin or use a strap.
- The straight knee may bend slightly; a blanket under the sit bones helps the pelvis tilt forward.
