**Thigh on the upper arm, gaze to the other side.**
If the shin comes easily to the forehead, thread the same-side hand inside the leg and press it into the mat, fingers pointing in, thigh resting on the upper arm; the other hand holds the outside of the foot, slowly straighten the leg, gaze to the other side — compass, three breaths.
Key points:
- The higher the thigh sits on the upper arm (toward the shoulder), the easier it is to straighten the leg.
- The supporting hand presses firmly to keep you upright.
- Chest turns up, look out from under the top arm.
- If the leg doesn't straighten, keep the knee bent — the opening matters more than a straight leg.
