**Back to plow first, then roll down vertebra by vertebra.**
Bring the legs back over the head into plow, arms beside the body pressing down, then with core control roll the spine back onto the mat, legs last.
Key points:
- Keep the head on the floor — don't lift it to look.
- The slower you lower, the better for neck and lower back.
- Once down, fish pose or hugging the knees balances the neck flexion.
