**A standing pigeon.**
Place the outer ankle of one foot on the opposite thigh just above the knee; bend the standing leg and sit the hips back and down, hands together in front of the chest.
Key points:
- Flex the lifted foot to protect the knee; the ankle rests above the knee, never on the joint.
- Sit back like in chair pose, back long.
- Feel the stretch in the outer hip of the lifted leg.
- Once stable, fold a little further forward to deepen.
Easier: practise at a wall, or just lift the foot without sitting down.
