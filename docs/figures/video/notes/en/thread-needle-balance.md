**Balance challenge — step by step.**
While holding the needle you can deepen gradually: ① top hand to the opposite waist; ② stretch the leg on the same side back; ③ lift the back leg off the floor and hold the balance.
Key points:
- Make each stage stable before moving on; if the breath gets short, go back one stage.
- With the leg lifted, reaching long matters more than lifting high.
- The shoulder on the mat doesn't carry everything — supporting hand and knee share the load.
- Core engaged, pelvis level, don't roll open.
