**Use the weight of the hand, not its strength.**
Inhale, arms up. Exhale, one hand on the mat, the other hand over the head to the opposite ear, lengthen the side of the neck — 2 breaths. Then lower the gaze to stretch the back of the neck — 2 breaths. Change sides.
Key points:
- The hand only rests on the head; let the weight of the arm do the work, don't pull.
- Actively sink the shoulder on the stretched side — the further it moves from the ear, the deeper the stretch.
- Looking down means turning the chin gently toward the armpit.
- To come out, guide the head back to center with the hand before letting go.
