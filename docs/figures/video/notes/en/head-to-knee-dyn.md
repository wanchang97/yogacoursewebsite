**Don't rush to grab the foot — let the breath move you.**
Bend one leg, sole against the inner thigh of the other leg. Inhale, arms up; exhale, turn toward the straight leg. Inhale, reach up again; exhale, the hands lead the upper body forward and down, gaze toward the toes. Move with the breath a few times.
Key points:
- Lengthen on every inhale; only on the exhale go a little further.
- Chest faces the straight leg, navel in line with the knee.
- The movement lets the back of the leg release gradually — don't dive to the deepest point every time.
