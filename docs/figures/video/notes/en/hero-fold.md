**The start and end point of cat–cow.**
From tabletop, sit back slowly toward the heels, hands stay in front, arms reach forward, forehead rests on the mat.
Key points:
- Hands press and arms lengthen while the hips sink back — the body is drawn apart from both ends.
- Knees together or mat-wide so the belly can rest between the thighs.
- If the hips don't reach the heels, place a blanket between thighs and calves.
- Feel the breath in the back: on the inhale the back widens up and out.
