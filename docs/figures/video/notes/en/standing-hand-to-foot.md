**Stable first, then extend.**
If your balance is steady, hold the big toe of the lifted leg with the hand on the same side; inhale, slowly extend the leg forward.
Key points:
- Hold the toe with the knee bent, then straighten bit by bit — if it doesn't straighten, keep the knee bent.
- The back stays upright; don't round to straighten the leg.
- The other hand on the hip helps you feel whether the pelvis is level.
- Use a strap if you can't reach the toe.
