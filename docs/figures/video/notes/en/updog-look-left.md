**In up-dog, look to one side.**
Once steady in up-dog, gently turn the head and look over the shoulder toward the heel on the same side.
Key points:
- Only the head and gaze turn; the chest keeps facing forward, the shoulders don't drop.
- The supporting hands keep pressing, shoulders away from the ears.
- Turn only as far as the neck is comfortable — this wakes up awareness of neck and side body.
