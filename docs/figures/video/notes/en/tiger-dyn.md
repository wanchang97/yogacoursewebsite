**Inhale lift the leg, exhale knee to nose.**
With the back leg extended, inhale and lift it. Exhale, bend the knee and draw it toward the nose, rounding the back. Three times.
Key points:
- Lift the leg only to the line of the body — don't sink into the lower back to lift higher.
- On the exhale round the back like in cat, navel in; knee and nose meet in the middle.
- Hands keep pressing, shoulders over the wrists, the body doesn't sway.
- The slower you follow the breath, the more you feel the core working.
