**Inhale to grow tall, exhale to lengthen sideways.**
Inhale, lift both arms in front of you and up. Exhale, one hand to the mat, the other arm over the ear, lengthen this side of the body. Stay 1–2 breaths, feel the side ribs open with the breath, then change sides.
Key points:
- First grow long, then bend — the side bend is the result of a long spine, not of collapsing the waist.
- Both sit bones stay grounded; the opposite hip does not lift.
- The top arm is beside the ear, the shoulder sinks away from the ear.
- Breathe into the lengthened side and feel the ribs expand and release.
