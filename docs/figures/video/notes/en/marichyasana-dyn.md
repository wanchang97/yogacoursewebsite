**Hug the knee and sit tall, then turn back.**
Inhale, sit upright. Exhale, bring the bent leg in, sole outside the knee of the straight leg. Inhale, sit tall and hug the bent knee; exhale, twist back toward that side.
Key points:
- Hugging the knee to the chest helps the spine sit taller.
- Each inhale grow taller; each exhale turn a little more at that height.
- The hand behind you presses near the sacrum to keep you upright, not leaning back.
