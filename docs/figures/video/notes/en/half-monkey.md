**Back knee under the hip, front leg straight.**
Hips stay right over the back knee, front leg straight, toes flexed; fold forward from the hips and stay a few breaths.
Key points:
- The back thigh is vertical, hips directly over the knee.
- Belly to thigh first; keep the back long instead of rounding and forcing.
- The front knee may be slightly bent — feel the stretch in the middle of the hamstring, not behind the knee.
- Both hips face forward; draw the hip of the front leg back.
