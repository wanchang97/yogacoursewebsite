**The weight is on the shoulders, not the neck.**
Feet rest on the floor behind the head, legs straight; stay a few breaths.
Key points:
- Weight on the shoulders and upper back; keep a little space behind the neck.
- If the feet don't reach the floor, support them on a block or chair, or bend the knees near the forehead.
- You may feel slight pressure in the throat — slow and soften the breath.
- Not recommended during menstruation, with neck problems or high blood pressure.
