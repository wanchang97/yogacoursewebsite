**One full vinyasa: one breath, one movement.**
Inhale, arms up. Exhale, fold, hands beside the feet, knees soft. Inhale, lengthen the front. Exhale, step back into plank, shoulders over the wrists. Inhale, shift forward. Exhale, elbows close — chaturanga. Inhale, up-dog. Exhale, down dog, three deep breaths.
Key points:
- First make each pose stable on its own (see the cards in this section), then link them.
- Inhale = opening, rising; exhale = folding, lowering. Remember this and you won't get lost.
- The breath sets the pace: if you can't keep up, take an extra breath in plank or down dog.
- Every step has an easier option: bent knees in the fold, kneeling plank, knees-down chaturanga, cobra instead of up-dog.
