**Cradle the leg and rock gently — preparing the hip.**
Back to sitting, hug one leg with both arms (shin across the chest, like cradling a baby) and rock gently up and down or side to side.
Key points:
- The shin is as parallel to the floor as possible; hold foot and knee together, don't pull only on the foot.
- Back upright — the leg comes to the chest, not the body bending down to the leg.
- Feel the hip joint relax as you rock, preparing for half lotus or twists.
- If the knee feels any pressure, lower the leg a little.
