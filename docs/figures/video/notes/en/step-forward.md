**Step forward: belly in, knee to chest.**
Exhale, bend the lifted leg, draw the knee to the chest and step forward between the hands.
Key points:
- Shoulders move forward over the wrists first — then the leg has room.
- Belly in and back slightly rounded help the knee come close to the chest.
- If one step doesn't make it, help the ankle forward with the hand or step in two moves.
- The front foot lands between the hands, shin as vertical as possible.
