**A deep hip opener.**
Bring both hands inside the front foot; if possible, lower the forearms to the mat — lizard, three deep breaths.
Key points:
- Move the front foot slightly outward to make room; the front knee may open a little.
- If the forearms don't reach, put a block under them or stay on the hands.
- The back leg may be straight or the knee down so the hips can sink.
- Let each exhale soften hips and groin — don't push down with force.
