**The hands become new "feet" — build the foundation first.**
Hands under the shoulders, knees under the hips. Spread the fingers, press the finger roots and the base of the thumb into the floor, rotate the arms from inside to outside. Draw the navel back and up so the back becomes long and flat.
Key points:
- Index fingers point forward; spread the weight over the whole hand, especially the roots of index finger and thumb — don't sink into the wrists.
- Outward rotation of the upper arms: the elbow creases turn slightly forward, the shoulders support the body fully.
- Belly gently engaged, the back is a flat table top.
- The neck continues the spine; gaze slightly in front of the hands.
Easier: if the wrists complain, make fists or place a folded blanket under the heels of the hands.
