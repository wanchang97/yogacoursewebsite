**The first inhale of the sun salutation.**
From mountain pose, inhale, sweep the arms up overhead; the whole body lengthens from the soles to the fingertips.
Key points:
- Feet keep rooting — the higher the arms go, the more the feet press down.
- Don't hunch: shoulder blades slide down, arms beside the ears.
- Ribs don't flare: draw the lower ribs in slightly instead of arching back to reach higher.
- Make this inhale full and slow, preparing the forward fold.
