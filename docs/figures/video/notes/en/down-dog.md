**Sit bones up and back, work from the back.**
Exhale, tuck the toes, push the sit bones up and back into down dog. Stay for three to five deep breaths.
Key points:
- Inhale: hands press, spine lengthens. Exhale: belly empties, navel toward the spine.
- Hands firm, upper arms rotate out so the shoulders carry the body fully.
- A long back is the priority — bend the knees if needed; heels sink toward the floor but don't have to touch.
- The body forms an inverted V; the head hangs relaxed between the arms.
