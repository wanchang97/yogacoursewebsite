**Shoulders past the wrists first, elbows close as you lower.**
Inhale, shift the weight forward until the shoulders are past the wrists. Exhale, elbows close to the body, lower the upper body as one unit about halfway down, elbows around 90 degrees.
Key points:
- "Forward first, then down" — without shifting forward the elbows can't reach 90° and the load goes into the front of the shoulders.
- Elbows hug the body and point back, not out.
- The whole body lowers like one plank — not chest first, not hips first.
- Feel the chest opening; the chin may move slightly forward.
- Stop at the height you can control — no need to reach the floor.
Easier: knees down — first the knees, then lower the upper body as one unit, elbows close, about 90°. Practise with 80 % effort; the easier version is practice too.
