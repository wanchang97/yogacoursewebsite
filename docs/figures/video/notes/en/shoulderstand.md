**One line from shoulders to toes.**
Legs together and straight up, body as vertical as possible; stay a few breaths.
Key points:
- Keep walking the hands toward the shoulders to straighten the back.
- Legs together, toes reaching, inner thighs gently squeezing.
- Weight on shoulders and upper arms, space behind the neck, don't turn the head.
- Not recommended during menstruation, with neck problems or high blood pressure; legs-up-the-wall is an alternative.
