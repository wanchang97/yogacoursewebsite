**The core carries the legs over the head.**
Lie on your back, arms beside the body, palms down. Inhale, lift the legs; exhale, use the belly to lift the hips off the floor and bring the legs over the head, toes toward the floor behind you.
Key points:
- The core leads — no swinging the legs with momentum.
- Once there, don't turn the head; the neck stays centred.
- Hands press the floor or interlace behind the back to draw the shoulders in and lift the upper back.
- Stop at any pressure in the neck; a folded blanket under the shoulders (head off the blanket) helps.
