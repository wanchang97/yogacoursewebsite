**Build the triangle base first, then walk in.**
Kneel, forearms down, fingers interlaced, elbows shoulder-width; crown between the hands, back of the head against the hands. Straighten the legs and walk the feet in until the hips are over the shoulders; bend the knees to lift the feet, then slowly straighten the legs up.
Key points:
- Forearms and elbows are the main support; the head carries very little — keep pressing the forearms and lifting the shoulders away from the ears.
- When the hips are over the shoulders, the legs become light by themselves.
- Lift with the core — no jumping, no kicking.
- Beginners practise at a wall; no headstand with neck problems, high blood pressure or during menstruation.
