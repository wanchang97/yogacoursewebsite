**Inhale to open, exhale to thread through.**
From tabletop, inhale, lift one arm and open this side of the chest. Exhale, thread the arm under the other one, shoulder down to the mat. Three times; the last time you can stay for three breaths.
Key points:
- On the inhale the gaze follows the fingertips; the rotation comes from the thoracic spine, not the lower back.
- On the exhale shoulder and cheek rest on the mat; hips stay above the knees.
- The supporting hand keeps pressing to give the upper back room to rotate.
- While holding, breathe into the space between the shoulder blades.
