**Hand to the floor, gaze up.**
You can bring the lower hand to the mat or a block outside the front foot and turn the gaze toward the top hand.
Key points:
- With the hand down, front knee and upper arm press into each other — this helps the chest turn open.
- If the hand doesn't reach, use a block — an open chest matters more than the hand on the floor.
- When looking up, the neck follows the spine; otherwise look to the side.
