Hands and feet press, arms straight, chest opens forward; stay a few breaths.
Key points:
- Slowly shift the weight toward the hands so the chest moves past the wrists — the backbend spreads over the whole back, not just the lower back.
- Feet grounded, inner thighs rotate slightly in, knees don't open.
- The head hangs naturally, neck relaxed.
- Keep breathing; if the breath shortens, come down.
