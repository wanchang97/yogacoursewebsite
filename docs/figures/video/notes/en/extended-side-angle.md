**One diagonal line from heel to fingertips.**
From Warrior II, inhale, shift the weight toward the front leg; exhale, forearm on the front thigh, top arm extends in one line with the body.
Key points:
- The outer edge of the back foot presses; one long diagonal from back heel to top fingertips.
- The forearm only rests on the thigh — legs and core carry the body.
- Chest turns up, top shoulder opens back, nothing collapses.
- Front knee over the ankle, pressing toward the little-toe side.
