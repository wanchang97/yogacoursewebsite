**Lower slowly, chin tucked.**
Tuck the chin, bend the elbows, bring the back of the head or upper back to the mat first, then roll the spine down vertebra by vertebra, hips last.
Key points:
- Tuck the chin first so the crown doesn't hit the floor.
- Back on the floor, knees together or hug the knees to release the lower back for a few breaths.
- Don't get up right away — a gentle supine twist balances the backbend.
