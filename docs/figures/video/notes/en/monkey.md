**Slide slowly, supported by blocks.**
From half splits, slide the front foot forward and the back leg back, letting the pelvis gradually sink toward the floor, hands on blocks beside you.
Key points:
- Pelvis faces straight forward; back knee and top of the back foot face down, not rolling out.
- A block under the buttocks gives you something to "sit" on — don't hang in the air forcing it.
- The hands carry part of the weight; sink slowly — any sharp sensation means back off right away.
- Front foot flexed, back instep pressing; both legs actively reach.
Easier: stay in half splits, or prop the hips higher.
