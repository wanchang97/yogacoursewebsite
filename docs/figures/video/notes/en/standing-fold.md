**Uttanasana: fold from the hips.**
Exhale, fold forward from the hips, hands beside the feet, knees soft. Inhale, press hands into the floor or shins, lift the head and lengthen the front body.
Key points:
- The hinge is in the hips, not the waist — the belly comes toward the thighs first.
- Bend the knees as much as you need for the hands to reach the floor without rounding the back.
- On the half lift the spine becomes a flat back, ready to step back.
- Weight toward the balls of the feet, not all in the heels.
