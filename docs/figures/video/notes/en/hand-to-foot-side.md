**Open to the side, gaze to the opposite side.**
Inhale, open the extended leg to the side, gaze to the opposite side, two breaths.
Key points:
- When the leg opens, the standing hip stays steady — the whole pelvis doesn't swing open.
- Open the leg first; turn the gaze only once you're stable.
- To come back, bring the leg to the front first, then bend the knee, release and lower slowly.
