**Press the tops of the feet, chest forward and up.**
Inhale, press the tops of the feet into the mat, push the hands down, lift the upper body, chest opens forward and up — upward-facing dog. Stretch the whole front body.
Key points:
- The tops of the feet and the hands are the only support; thighs and knees lift off the floor if possible.
- Hands press and fingers grip; collarbones widen, shoulder blades slide down the back.
- Shoulders away from the ears — if they hunch, the hands are too far forward or not pressing enough.
- The backbend comes from opening the upper back; the belly draws in slightly and the pubic bone moves forward to protect the lower back.
Easier: cobra, thighs stay on the floor.
