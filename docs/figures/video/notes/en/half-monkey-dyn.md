**Rock back and forth to wake up the hamstrings.**
Back knee down, sit the hips back over the back knee, front leg straight, toes flexed; then move forward into the lunge again. Go back and forth with the breath a few times.
Key points:
- Moving back, inhale to lengthen the spine; exhale to fold from the hips.
- The aim is to warm the back of the leg gradually — not to go deep right away.
- Hands can rest on blocks beside the front leg to keep the back flat.
