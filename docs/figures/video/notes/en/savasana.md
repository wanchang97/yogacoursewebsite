**There is nothing to do.**
Slowly extend the legs, arms beside the body, palms up — savasana (corpse pose). Let the breath flow naturally and relax every part of the body — toes, legs, belly, chest, shoulders, hands, face. Stay until the music fades.
Key points:
- Feet fall open, arms a little away from the body so the shoulders relax completely.
- If the lower back is uncomfortable, put a rolled blanket under the knees.
- No more controlling the breath — just observe.
- To come out, move fingers and toes, roll to one side, then sit up slowly.
