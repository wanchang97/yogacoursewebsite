**Arms back, level with the leg.**
From Warrior I, inhale and shift the weight onto the front foot; exhale, slowly lift the back leg into Warrior III; inhale, sweep the arms back to leg height — airplane, two breaths.
Key points:
- Standing leg slightly bent, the three points of the foot rooted.
- The lifted leg forms one line with the body; hips face down and stay level, not opening.
- Arms back, palms down; the chest opens slightly forward.
- Gaze at a fixed point on the floor ahead to help balance.
Easier: back toes touch the floor, or hands on blocks.
