**Fold forward on top of the opening.**
Once stable in the splits, inhale to lengthen the spine, exhale and fold toward the front leg.
Key points:
- Only fold when the splits themselves are stable and the breath calm.
- Belly toward thigh first, back long.
- Hands keep supporting on the blocks.
- To come out, push up with the hands, bring the back knee in, then slowly draw the front leg back.
