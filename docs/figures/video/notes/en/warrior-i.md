**Reach up, root down.**
Inhale, lift the leg; exhale, step the foot between the hands. Inhale, arms up, high lunge. Front shin vertical, groin not compressed, weight evenly shared. The back heel may stay lifted or come down. Warrior I, five breaths. When stable, slowly sink until the thigh is parallel to the floor.
Key points:
- Front knee over the ankle, pointing toward the second and third toes.
- Pelvis faces forward as much as possible; draw the hip of the back leg forward.
- Inhale — feel the body grow up from the midline; exhale — root deeper into the feet.
- Image: a small boat on a lake following the waves — not complete stillness, but finding stability within the movement.
Easier: back knee down, low lunge.
