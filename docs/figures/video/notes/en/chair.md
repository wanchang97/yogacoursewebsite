**Sit down like a wave.**
From mountain pose, as the arms rise, soften the hips, bend the knees and lower the body, as if sitting on an invisible chair.
Key points:
- No arched lower back, no sticking the bottom out: tailbone down, belly gently in.
- Knees don't go past the toes; weight toward the heels — you can still see your toes.
- Knees together or hip-width, not falling inward.
- Balance practice: lift one heel, then both heels, then lower them.
