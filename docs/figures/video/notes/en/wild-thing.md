**From reverse tabletop flip open to the side, heart up.**
Inhale, one hand presses, the other arm reaches out to the side, legs stay, the body opens to the side — wild thing, three times dynamically. The last time stay up and let the top hand travel toward the floor.
Key points:
- The supporting hand presses firmly, shoulder over the wrist, no collapsing.
- Hips push up — chest and hips open up together into a backbend.
- The top arm leads the body long; the head follows and relaxes.
- In the dynamic version: inhale to open, exhale back to reverse tabletop.
