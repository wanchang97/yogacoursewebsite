**Preparing to step forward.**
From down dog, inhale and lift one leg back and up.
Key points:
- The lifted leg forms one long line from the wrists to the heel — length first, then height.
- Keep the hips level and facing down at first; to open the hip, bend the knee and open to the side.
- Both hands press evenly; don't shift the weight onto one hand.
- On the next exhale, draw the knee to the chest and step between the hands.
