# Week 7-8: Space & Backbend – Balance Challenge

**Introduction**: Moving into the second phase of the course. Having built a solid standing foundation, we now challenge single-leg balance — Warrior III, airplane, and Eagle Pose. The core of balance poses isn't "not falling" — it's finding inner stillness within dynamic movement.

---

## A. Core Theme: Dynamic Balance

### What Balance Really Means

In single-leg balance, the body is always making tiny adjustments. This "imperfection" is the heart of the practice:
- **Don't try to "freeze" yourself** — allow micro-movements
- **The breath is the anchor** — when you wobble, don't hold your breath; a deep breath actually helps you find center again
- **The gaze is a tool** — find a fixed point on the floor to help focus

> 💡 Like surfing on water — don't resist the waves; flow with them and find balance within.

## B. Balance Challenge Sequence (Segment 6) (🎵 Music 4)

### Transition: Sun Salutation

From Mountain Pose, activate the whole body with one sun salutation round before the balance challenge:

**Mountain** → **Inhale Chair** → **Exhale Forward Fold** → **Inhale Lift Head** → **Exhale Step to Plank** → **Inhale Shift Forward** → **Exhale Chaturanga** → **Inhale Cobra/Up Dog** → **Exhale Down Dog**, 2 deep breaths.

### Warrior III → Airplane Warrior (Left)

**Inhale** lift left leg back; **exhale** step left foot between hands. **Inhale** arms up — **Warrior I**, 2 breaths.

→ **Inhale** shift weight to front foot ball; **exhale** slowly lift back foot:

| Level | Description | Hold |
|-------|-------------|------|
| Foundation | Lift back foot, knee bent | 2 breaths |
| Warrior III | Straighten back leg while extending front arms, body forms a T-shape | 2 breaths |
| Airplane Warrior III | **Inhale** swing both arms back to leg height | 2 breaths |

**Inhale** arms carry back leg to stand, arms lift high (leg bent or straight).

> **Advanced variation**: From standing, use same-side hand to grip big toe. Inhale slowly straighten leg forward; inhale again to open leg to the side, gaze turns to opposite side, hold 2 breaths.

### Hug Knees → Side Opening → Eagle Pose (Left)

**Inhale** hug both knees while standing. **Exhale** slowly extend right hand, gaze to fingertips — **right side stretch**, 2 breaths. **Inhale** come back to center.

Into **Eagle Pose**:
- **Inhale** arms forward at shoulder height; **right arm threads under left arm**, palms face each other
- **Exhale**: **right leg crosses over left thigh**, toes hook behind left calf
- **Bend knees and sit lower**, spine tall, elbows rise to shoulder height, fingertips point up
- **Hold 3 breaths**

### Transition to Other Side

**Inhale Chair** → **Exhale Forward Fold** → **Inhale Lift Head** → **Exhale Step Feet Back** → **Inhale Shift to Plank** → **Exhale Chaturanga** → **Inhale Up Dog** → **Exhale Down Dog**, 2 breaths.

Then **repeat on the right side**: Warrior I → Warrior III → Airplane → Hug Knees Side Stretch → Eagle (left/right swapped).

> **Optional addition**: If time allows, add an arm balance here such as Crow Pose (Bakasana).

## C. Pose Breakdown

### Warrior III (Virabhadrasana III)

**Key alignment**:
- Standing knee slightly bent, never locked
- Pelvis level (imagine a bowl of water on your pelvis — don't tip it)
- Back ankle, knee, hip, spine, crown of head all in one line

### Eagle Pose (Garudasana)

**Key alignment**:
- Both elbows lift up — don't let the arms sink
- As you sink lower, feel the sacrum drop while back stays tall
- If toes can't hook the calf, simply cross the leg — that's perfectly fine

## D. Practice Tools

### Checklist
- [ ] Warrior III: pelvis level, breathe without holding
- [ ] Eagle: feel the upper back/scapula stretch (shoulder blades move apart)
- [ ] When you fall out: smile, reset — this is the practice
