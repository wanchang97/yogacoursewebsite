# Week 5-6: Center & Fold – Standing Sequence (Right Side)

**Introduction**: This week we mirror the left-side standing sequence on the right. Through this symmetrical practice, develop body awareness — noticing which side feels more open, which feels tighter. This noticing itself is yoga.

---

## A. Core Theme: Symmetry & Awareness

### Why Both Sides?

Left and right are rarely equal:
- The dominant arm side often has tighter shoulders
- Long periods of sitting create hip imbalances

**Practice intention**: Don't try to "copy" the left side experience. **Arrive fresh on this side** and feel what's actually here.

---

## B. Standing Sequence – Right Side (Segment 5) (🎵 Music 3)

### Mountain Pose
Same as left: stable triangular foot foundation, active legs, level pelvis, tall spine. **Three deep breaths.**

### Forward Fold → Plank → Downward Dog

| Step | Breath | Movement |
|------|--------|----------|
| 1 | **Inhale** | Arms up |
| 2 | **Exhale** | Forward fold, hands by feet |
| 3 | **Inhale** | Lengthen front body |
| 4 | **Exhale** | Right, then left foot back to **Plank** |
| 5 | **Inhale** | Weight forward |
| 6 | **Exhale** | Hug elbows in — **Chaturanga** |
| 7 | **Inhale** | **Up Dog** |
| 8 | **Exhale** | **Down Dog**, 3 deep breaths |

### Warrior I (Right Side)
**Inhale** lift right leg; **exhale** step right foot between hands. **Inhale** arms up, High Lunge. Shin vertical, weight even.

**Warrior I — 5 breaths**, thigh slowly approaching parallel to floor.

### Warrior II → Reverse Warrior → Extended Side Angle (Right)

**Inhale** back foot 90°, gaze over right hand — **Warrior II**, 5 breaths.

→ **Inhale** body arches back — **Reverse Warrior**; return.

→ **Inhale** shift right; **exhale** right forearm on right thigh, left arm extends — **Extended Side Angle**.

*Advanced: right hand outside the foot; or bind hands behind back.*

### Triangle → Pyramid → Revolved Triangle (Right)

Straighten front leg, shorten stance.

- **Inhale** arms open, body extends right; **exhale** arms up and down — **Triangle**, 2 breaths
- **Inhale** face short end, shorten stance, arms up; **exhale** fold forward — **Pyramid**, 2 deep breaths
- **Inhale** left hand outside front foot, right arm lifts, gaze to right — **Revolved Triangle**

### Crescent Moon → Twist → Lizard (Right)

**Inhale** hands beside foot, back foot steps back. Lift torso — **Crescent Moon**, 3 breaths.

→ Hands together overhead → to chest → side twist, 2 breaths

→ Hands inside right foot, lower to elbows — **Lizard**, 3 deep breaths

→ Lower back knee, inner-rotate left forearm 90°, right arm up, right hand pushes right knee outward, 2 breaths

### Half Pigeon → Full Pigeon (Right)

**Inhale** front leg parallel to short edge — **Half Pigeon**, 5 deep breaths.

Back leg to side — **Full Pigeon**. **Inhale** arms up; **exhale** forward fold, 5 breaths.

Return to Down Dog, step forward, rise to standing.

---

## C. Noticing the Difference

After completing both sides, stand in Mountain Pose, close your eyes and notice:
- Do your hips feel more balanced?
- Which side's Reverse Warrior felt more free?
- Which Pigeon was deeper?

No analysis needed — just notice. Next practice, you can give the tighter side one extra breath.

---

## D. Practice Tools

### Checklist
- [ ] Compare Lizard on both sides: which hip flexor is tighter?
- [ ] Notice Triangle differences: hamstring vs. side waist
- [ ] After finishing: sit quietly for 2 minutes and feel how symmetry has shifted
