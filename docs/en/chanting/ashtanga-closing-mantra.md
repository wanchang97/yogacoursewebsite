# Ashtanga Closing Mantra

<p class="pose-sanskrit">阿斯汤加结束唱诵 · Maṅgala Mantra</p>

The auspicious prayer (Maṅgala Mantra) chanted at the end of an Ashtanga practice, after relaxation. While the opening mantra turns inward to honour the teachers, the closing mantra **sends the goodwill of the practice outward**: may the world be just, may all beings be happy — ending with peace three times.

## Sanskrit (Devanāgarī)

<div class="mantra"><div class="dev">ॐ<br>
स्वस्ति प्रजाभ्यः परिपालयन्तां<br>
न्यायेन मार्गेण महीं महीशाः ।<br>
गोब्राह्मणेभ्यः शुभमस्तु नित्यं<br>
लोकाः समस्ताः सुखिनो भवन्तु ॥<br>
ॐ शान्तिः शान्तिः शान्तिः</div></div>

## Transliteration (for chanting)

<div class="mantra"><div class="iast">oṁ<br>
svasti prajābhyaḥ paripālayantāṁ<br>
nyāyena mārgeṇa mahīṁ mahīśāḥ<br>
go-brāhmaṇebhyaḥ śubham astu nityaṁ<br>
lokāḥ samastāḥ sukhino bhavantu<br>
oṁ śāntiḥ śāntiḥ śāntiḥ</div></div>

## Line by line

| Sanskrit | Meaning |
|---|---|
| *svasti prajābhyaḥ paripālayantāṁ* | May all people be well and protected; |
| *nyāyena mārgeṇa mahīṁ mahīśāḥ* | may those who lead the earth act on the path of justice; |
| *go-brāhmaṇebhyaḥ śubham astu nityaṁ* | may goodness always be with all that is sacred and with the wise; |
| *lokāḥ samastāḥ sukhino bhavantu* | may all the worlds, all beings, be happy. |
| *oṁ śāntiḥ śāntiḥ śāntiḥ* | Peace, peace, peace. |

**Three times śānti**: traditionally peace on three levels — within ourselves, with the people and surroundings around us, and with the forces of nature and the world beyond our control.

## Pronunciation tips

- **ḥ** (visarga) is a soft breath after the vowel, a faint echo of "h": *prajābhyaḥ* ≈ pra-jaa-bhya(h).
- **ś** is close to "sh": *śāntiḥ* ≈ shaan-ti(h).
- **ṇ / ṁ** are nasal; vowels with a line (ā ī) are long.

<div class="mantra-tip">
<b>Teaching tips</b><br>
· Chant it after savasana: roll to the side, come up to sitting, palms together at the heart.<br>
· One OM before and after; let the three śānti become softer and slower each time.<br>
· Sit for a few breaths afterwards, then close the class together with "Namaste".<br>
· Again: listening without chanting is completely fine.
</div>
