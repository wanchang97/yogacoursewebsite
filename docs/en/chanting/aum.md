# AUM Chanting

<p class="pose-sanskrit">AUM 唱诵 · ॐ</p>

AUM (OM) is the primordial sound in yoga — **A begins, U carries, M closes**, and then comes silence. It often opens or closes a class: it brings us from the busy day into the present moment and connects a whole room through one shared sound.

## Guidance

Come into a comfortable seat, spine upright, hands on the knees. Inhale; sound the AUM on the exhale:

| Sound | Share of time | Where it vibrates | Mouth |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A** ("ah") | ~30 % | pelvic floor and hips | mouth naturally open |
| **U** ("oo") | ~30 % | resonance rises into belly and chest | lips rounded |
| **M** ("mm") | ~40 % | a clear hum in the head | lips gently closed |

The M hums the longest and fades slowly — **silence is the fourth part of AUM**. Stay a moment after the sound and feel the vibration linger.

Let's chant AUM together three times.

<div class="mantra-tip">
<b>Teaching tips</b><br>
· Volume is not the goal; finishing the sound in one breath is. If the breath is short, shorten A and U and keep M long.<br>
· Everyone can chant at their own comfortable pitch — no need to match.<br>
· With beginners, explain briefly why we chant, then invite: anyone who prefers not to use their voice can simply listen and feel the vibration.
</div>
