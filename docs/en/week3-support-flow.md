# Week 3-4: Support & Flow – Tabletop Warmup & Standing Sequence (Left Side)

**Introduction**: We transition from the tabletop position into our first complete standing sequence. The key is maintaining the rooting sensation as we move from hands and feet on the ground to standing poses, using the breath to find extension and stability simultaneously.

---

## A. Core Theme: Support & Flow

### From Tabletop to Standing – Finding the Body's Centerline

Every standing pose has two simultaneous forces:
- **Rooting down** (exhale): feet and hands press into the earth, finding center of gravity
- **Rising up** (inhale): spine and crown of head grow upward from the centerline

> 💡 Imagine yourself as a small boat on a lake — the water moves, but your mast (spine) always remains vertical.

---

## B. Warm-Up Sequence (Segments 2-3)

### Seated Warm-Up

Before standing poses, awaken the body through seated movement:

| Movement | Breath | Duration |
|----------|--------|----------|
| Lateral stretch (both sides) | Inhale arms up, exhale side bend | 1-2 breaths |
| Neck stretch (both sides) | Inhale arms up, exhale tilt head | 2 breaths, deepen with gaze down |
| Seated twist (both sides) | Inhale lengthen, exhale twist | 1-2 breaths |

### Tabletop Warm-Up (🎵 Music 1)

**Establishing Tabletop Position**
- Hands directly under shoulders, knees under hips
- **Spread fingers wide, press the webbing and finger roots into the mat**; arms rotate slightly outward
- **Draw the navel back and up**, letting the back lengthen and flatten

**Cat-Cow Dynamic** (3 rounds)
- Inhale: Cat (gaze to navel, slide forward into tabletop)
- Exhale: Cow (lift head, return to Child's Pose)

**Threading the Needle (both sides)**
- Inhale arm up to open chest; exhale shoulder to mat
- Hold 3 breaths; advanced: deepen with opposite hand on waist, back leg extending

**Tiger Pose (both sides)**
- Inhale leg up, exhale knee to nose (3 times)
- Then: side bend standing (3 times), grab ankle to open front body (hold 2 breaths)

**First Downward Dog Transition**
- Build hand support, lift both knees slightly off the ground (2 breaths)
- Extend both legs into Downward Dog (3 deep breaths)
- Walk feet toward hands, slowly rise to standing

## C. Standing Sequence – Left Side (Segment 4) (🎵 Music 2)

### Mountain Pose
Heel, ball of little toe, ball of big toe ground down. **Build a stable triangular foundation**. Activate legs inner, outer, and back. Hips level, spine tall, shoulders roll down and back.

🔑 **Three deep breaths** — let the body truly "arrive" at this starting point.

### Forward Fold → Plank → Downward Dog (Sun Salutation)

| Step | Breath | Movement |
|------|--------|----------|
| 1 | **Inhale** | Arms sweep forward and up |
| 2 | **Exhale** | Forward fold, hands beside feet (knees soft) |
| 3 | **Inhale** | Press hands, lengthen front body |
| 4 | **Exhale** | Step left, then right foot back to **Plank** |
| 5 | **Inhale** | Shift weight forward, shoulders over wrists |
| 6 | **Exhale** | Hug elbows in, lower as one line to 90°—**Chaturanga** (shoulders no lower than elbows) |
| 7 | **Inhale** | Press up—**Upward Dog** |
| 8 | **Exhale** | From the back body, press to **Downward Dog** — 3 deep breaths |

### Warrior I
**Inhale** lift left leg; **exhale** step left foot between hands. **Inhale** arms up into **High Lunge**.

**5-breath holding — imagery cues:**
- ① Inhale: feel the body extend upward from the centerline; exhale: root deeper
- ③ Inhale: like a small boat on a lake following the waves; exhale: find stillness in movement
- ⑤ Inhale…exhale: feel the stability and balance of your centerline

*When stable: slowly allow the thigh to become parallel to the floor.*

### Warrior II → Reverse Warrior → Extended Side Angle

**Inhale** rotate back foot 90°, open body to the side, gaze over left hand — **Warrior II**, 5 breaths.

→ **Inhale** legs stay, body arches back — **Reverse Warrior**; return to Warrior II.

→ **Inhale** shift weight left; **exhale** left forearm on left thigh, right arm extends to a long diagonal line — **Extended Side Angle**.

*Advanced: left hand outside the foot; deeper: bind hands behind back.*

### Triangle → Pyramid → Revolved Triangle

Straighten the front leg, shorten stance slightly.

- **Inhale** arms open, body extends left to limit; **exhale** arms reach up and down — **Triangle**, 2 breaths
- **Inhale** turn to face short end of mat, shorten stance further, arms up; **exhale** forward fold — **Pyramid**, 2 deep breaths
- **Inhale** left hand outside front foot, right arm lifts, gaze toward right hand — **Revolved Triangle**

### Crescent Moon → Twist → Lizard

**Inhale** hands beside front foot, back foot steps back, top of foot presses down. Lift torso — **Crescent Moon**, 3 breaths.

*Front shin vertical; press front ball of foot and back instep, relieving the back knee.*

→ Hands together overhead → to chest → twist to the side (2 breaths)

→ Hands inside left foot, lower to elbows — **Lizard Pose**, 3 deep breaths

→ Lower back knee, inner-rotate right forearm 90°, extend left arm up, left hand pushes left knee outward (2 breaths)

### Half Pigeon → Full Pigeon

**Inhale** bring front leg parallel to the short edge — **Half Pigeon**, 5 deep breaths.

Front leg stays; bring back leg to the side — **Full Pigeon**.

**Inhale** arms up; **exhale** forward fold — *maintain space in the front body, gaze forward first; once belly and chest come closer, lower the gaze* — relax 5 breaths.

Press hands to Downward Dog, step forward, slowly rise — prepare for the right side.

## D. Teaching Notes (Three-Round Flow)

### Round 1: Detailed Breakdown
- Key cues: "Inhale extend up, exhale root down" in each pose
- In Warrior I: don't rush to deepen the lunge; first feel the breath in the chest and belly

### Round 2: Flow with Music
- Reduce anatomical language, let movement follow the music rhythm
- Give only directional cues: "Inhale arms up, exhale forward fold"

### Round 3: Self-Exploration
- Close eyes, repeat at your own breath pace
- Advanced: try bound variations, extend holds
