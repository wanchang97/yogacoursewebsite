## B. Seated Stretching Sequence (Segments 7-8) (🎵 Respite)

### Seated Sequence – Left Side

#### Head-to-Knee Pose (Janu Sirsasana)

Sit on the mat, bend the right leg, right sole against the inner left thigh.

- **Inhale** arms up; **exhale** rotate body toward the extended left leg
- **Inhale** continue reaching up; **exhale** hands bring the torso **forward and down**, gaze toward the toes first
- **Don't rush to grab the foot — let the breath lead the body**; gradually bring right hand to the outer left foot
- Feel where it pulls the most — the back of the straight leg or the bent leg's calf
- **Hold for 5 deep breaths**

#### Marichyasana Twist

**Inhale** sit tall. **Exhale** rotate the bent leg inward 90°, sole beside the outer left knee.

- **Inhale** tall, hug the right knee with both arms
- **Exhale** twist **to the right and behind**
- Challenge: extend left arm outside right knee, or wrap around right thigh and bind hands behind back — **Marichyasana**
- **Hold 3 breaths**

#### Revolved Head-to-Knee

Return to sitting, rock the right leg gently, then place foot inside left thigh.

- **Inhale** rotate right; **exhale** right hand finds left waist, can bind with foot
- **Inhale** extend left arm; **exhale** left arm leads the torso **forward and down**, right knee sinks toward floor
- **Hold 5 breaths**

#### Compass Pose (Parivrtta Surya Yantrasana)

> *Advanced variation — practice within your own range*

**Inhale** hold the right heel, slowly extend the right knee, torso upright.

1. **Bring shin and forehead toward each other three times** (feel the hamstring stretch)
2. If this comes easily: right hand threads under the leg to press the mat, fingers facing inward, thigh rests on upper arm
3. Left hand grips outer foot, slowly straighten leg, gaze turns left — **Compass Pose**
4. **Hold 3 breaths**

#### Reverse Tabletop → Wild Thing

Slowly lower the right leg, right foot on the floor, press both hands behind you, lift the body — **Reverse Tabletop**, 2 breaths.

**Inhale** press left hand, right arm sweeps to side, legs stay, body opens to the left — **Wild Thing**, 3 dynamic rounds.

Last round: stay lifted, right hand reaches toward the floor.

## C. Final Relaxation – Savasana (Segment 9) (🎵 Softhearted)

Come to sitting, **bring the soles of the feet together, knees open** — **Bound Angle Pose**.

Slowly lie back — **Reclined Bound Angle**, arms relaxed beside the body, palms facing up. Hold 3 breaths, release.

Then slowly extend both legs — **Savasana (Corpse Pose)**.

Let the breath flow naturally. **Completely relax every part of the body**:
- From the toes… legs… abdomen…
- Chest… shoulders… hands…
- Neck… face…
- Remain until the music fades.

> 🌙 Savasana is not "sleeping" — it's **conscious total relaxation**. This is the convergence point of all the effort in the session, and the moment the body integrates everything practiced.
