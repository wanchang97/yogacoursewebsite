# ☀️ Summer Course · 5 Sessions (Compact Course)

**Introduction**: This compact course continues the Vinyasa flow sequence, but teaches and explores it in finer segments — spread across **five 75-minute sessions**. We take the familiar sequence apart piece by piece, go deep into one segment each session, and put it back together as one whole flow at the end. The full video with music is on the [Home page](/en/README.md).

> Attitude: sharing, not a test. 80% effort, in dialogue with the body. → [☯️ What is Yoga](/en/philosophy.md)

---

## A. The Course Model

**Four phases every session** — so each class is full, but never overpacked:
Warm-up (breath · seated warm-up · tabletop) → **Flow** (standing & balance) → **Cool-down** (seated stretch) → **Close** (final relaxation · chanting).

**One segment taught in detail per session.** The deep-teaching works like the *three rounds* in the weekly chapters: explain once without music, practise once with music. A detailed pass costs a good 2× its music length — so we only go deep into **one** major segment per session.

**Modular music.** The sequence is made of separate tracks (see Section C). On teaching days you play only the tracks you're *reviewing*, and replace the new segment with the slow spoken pass. The complete, uninterrupted flow is saved for **Session 5**.

**Time anchor:** tabletop → final relaxation with music ≈ **50 minutes**. 75 − 50 ≈ **25 minutes** of room each session for breath, warm-up, chanting and *one* detail focus.

## B. The Five Sessions

### Session 1 · Foundations: Breath & Tabletop
- **Focus / detail**: seated breathing and the tabletop sequence.
- **Segments**: [Week 1: Breathing & Space](/en/week1-breathing.md) · [Week 2: Grounding & Tabletop](/en/week2-tabletopsequence.md)
- **Music**: Track 1 *flow_warmup* (7:06) · Track 7 *Savasana* (3:46)
- **Flow of the class**: overview & sign-in (today only) → breath & seated warm-up → tabletop in detail → short relaxation → final relaxation · chanting.

### Session 2 · Standing Sequence
- **Focus / detail**: the standing sequence (warrior, triangle, half-moon, lizard, pigeon).
- **Segments**: [Week 3–4: Support & Flow](/en/week3-support-flow.md)
- **Music**: Track 1 (review, 7:06) → slow spoken pass, one side → Track 2 (9:51) → Track 3 *other side* (9:51) → Track 7.
- **Flow of the class**: warm-up → **flow (detail)** → short cool-down → close.

### Session 3 · Balance
- **Focus / detail**: the standing balance poses (Warrior III, Airplane, Eagle).
- **Segments**: [Week 7–8: Balance Challenge](/en/week7-space-backbend.md)
- **Music**: Track 1 → Track 2 (one side, review) → slow spoken pass, balance → Track 4 *flow_cooldown* (7:59) → Track 5 (5:29, first taste) → Track 7.
- **Flow of the class**: warm-up → flow → **balance (detail)** → cool-down → close.
- *Note: go deep only on balance this session — the seated stretch comes in Session 4, so the class doesn't get overloaded.*

### Session 4 · Seated Stretch & First Full Link
- **Focus / detail**: the seated-stretch sequence (head-to-knee, Marichyasana, compass, reverse tabletop).
- **Segments**: [Week 5–6: Center & Fold](/en/week5-center-fold.md) · [Week 9–10: Twist, Seated & Savasana](/en/week9-twist-vinyasa.md)
- **Music**: Tracks 1 → 2 → 4 (link the front half with music) → slow spoken pass, seated stretch → Track 5 (+ 6) → Track 7.
- **Flow of the class**: warm-up → flow → **cool-down (detail)** → close. Then room for first attempts: backbend & shoulderstand (with variations).

### Session 5 · With Music — the Whole Sequence
- **Focus**: once from start to finish, continuously with music.
- **Segments**: all · [Full Flow Video](/en/README.md)
- **Music**: **separate tracks 1 → 4** continuously, then pause briefly for backbend & shoulderstand, then Tracks 5, 6 → Track 7. (The merged file is for uninterrupted self-practice at home.)
- **Flow of the class**: all four phases, with no new detail teaching.

## D. Rhythm & Attitude

Every session we repeat — with music — everything practised up to the previous session, so the body slowly grows a memory through repetition. Choose the review deliberately: one side is often enough; you don't have to play everything every time.

And: better to close 3–5 minutes early than to run over. A class that ends calm carries more than one packed to the brim. The final relaxation always gets its time.

> **Practice tool**: between sessions — sit for 3 minutes a day and just watch the breath. As long as you can breathe, you can do yoga.
