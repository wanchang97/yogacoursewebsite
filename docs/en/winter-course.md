# ❄️ Winter Semester · WiSe 2026/27 (14 sessions × 75 min)

**When**: Thursdays **4:00–5:15 PM**, 22 October 2026 – 4 February 2027 (Christmas break: no class on 24 Dec and 31 Dec)
**Where**: Bauhaus Unisport · see your registration for the room

**Introduction**: The winter semester brings a new university sport course. We start again from the breath and — as in spring — build the whole Vinyasa sequence one theme per week. From the summer compact course we keep the structure: every 75-minute session has **warm-up → flow → cool-down → close**. In the last class before Christmas we practise the whole sequence with music for the first time; after New Year we go a little deeper — backbends, first inversions — and close on 4 February with the full flow. The full video with music is on the [Home page](/en/README.md).

> Attitude: sharing, not a test. 80% effort, in dialogue with the body. → [☯️ What is Yoga](/en/philosophy.md)

## A. The Course Model

**Four phases every session**: Warm-up (breath · seated warm-up · tabletop) → **Flow** (standing & balance) → **Cool-down** (seated stretch) → **Close** (final relaxation · chanting).

**A little more warm-up in winter.** Cold days, straight from the desk: the body needs longer to wake up. We give joints and spine 3–5 extra minutes before we come to standing.

**One theme in detail per session.** Following the *three rounds* of the weekly chapters: slow explanation without music → practice with music → free exploration. What we already know is reviewed briefly with music (often one side is enough).

**Three phases across the semester:**
1. **Foundations (sessions 1–4)**: breath, tabletop, standing poses.
2. **Completing the sequence (sessions 5–9)**: forward folds, balance, twists & seated stretch — the whole flow for the first time before Christmas.
3. **A little deeper (sessions 10–14)**: back on the mat after the break, backbends and first inversions, and the full flow to finish.

### Focus of each session

**Session 1 · Breathing & Space** — overview, sign-up, forms (today only). Quiet breathing, seated warm-up, a first taste of tabletop; final relaxation and chanting. What sets yoga apart from other sport: *space*.

**Session 2 · Tabletop** — support of hands and feet, cat-cow, thread-the-needle, tiger. Track 1 *flow_warmup* with music for the first time.

**Session 3 · Standing poses** — mountain, triangle, side angle, pyramid: grounded feet, active legs. Mostly slow, without music.

**Session 4 · Standing sequence & flow** — warrior, half-moon, lizard, pigeon in flow; track 2 (one side).

**Session 5 · Center & Fold** — back to the midline in forward folds; the mirror side with track 3.

**Session 6 · Balance I** — Warrior III, airplane: stability within movement, with wall or block.

**Session 7 · Balance II** — eagle; linking tabletop → standing → balance with music (tracks 1 → 2 → 4).

**Session 8 · Twist & seated stretch** — head-to-knee, Marichi twist, compass, reverse tabletop (tracks 5, 6); plenty of time for savasana.

**Session 9 · The whole thing, first time** — last class before Christmas, nothing new: from tabletop to final relaxation, continuously with music (about 50 min).

**Session 10 · Back on the mat** — after the break, first back to the breath. The whole sequence slowly with verbal cues; one side is enough.

**Session 11 · Backbends** — cobra, locust, bridge, and wheel for those who want it; a neutralising pose after every backbend.

**Session 12 · First inversions** — shoulderstand and plough, starting with wall and padding variations; with any neck discomfort, stay in the variation.

**Session 13 · Wish session** — based on feedback from the first twelve sessions, we refine the one or two poses or segments you most want.

**Session 14 · Semester close** — all four phases, the whole sequence with music, backbend and shoulderstand in a short pause before the cool-down; chanting together at the end.

## D. Rhythm & Attitude

It gets dark early in winter — by the time we finish at 5:15 PM it may already be night outside. Give yourself a warm hour: bring a warm jacket and cover yourself with it during final relaxation.

Better to finish 3–5 minutes early than to run over. Missing a session is no problem — every class reviews what came before with music, so you can join back in at any time.

> **Practice tool**: Between sessions — sit quietly for 3 minutes a day and simply watch the breath. As long as you can breathe, you can practise yoga.
