# Counting the Breath

<p class="pose-sanskrit">数呼吸 · Sankhya</p>

Counting gives the busy mind one very simple thing to do: it doesn't change the breath, it just **keeps the breath company**. Drifting off is fine — noticing and coming back is the practice itself.

First sit stable (see [Who breath · ocean breath](/en/pranayama/who-breath.md)) and breathe naturally through the nose or with ocean breath.

## Practice 1 · Count the exhales 1 → 10

<div class="br-box">
Inhale … exhale, silently count <b>“1”</b>.<br>
Inhale … exhale, <b>“2”</b>.<br>
… up to <b>10</b>, then start again at 1.<br>
If you lose count, go past 10 or notice you're thinking of something else — <b>no problem, gently return to 1</b>.
</div>

- Only count, don't control: the breath is as long as it is.
- Duration: 3–5 minutes. Good at the start of class to arrive on the mat.

## Practice 2 · Equal breathing (Sama Vṛtti)

<div class="br-box">
Inhale, count 1–2–3–4;<br>
exhale, count 1–2–3–4.<br>
Inhale and exhale the same length, even like a pendulum.
</div>

- If 4 counts feel too short or long, use 3 or 5 — find a length that is **effortless**.
- Effect: a steady, rhythmic breath — the basis for “one breath, one movement” in vinyasa.

## Practice 3 · Lengthening the exhale

<div class="br-box">
Inhale, count 1–2–3–4;<br>
exhale, count 1–2–3–4–5–6.<br>
Once steady, you can slowly extend the exhale to 8.
</div>

- A longer exhale helps the body relax — good at the end of class, before savasana, or before sleep.
- On the exhale imagine the body sinking down and toward the centre; shoulders, jaw and brow soften bit by bit.

<div class="br-tip">
<b>Teaching tips</b><br>
· No breath holds at beginner level — only work with the length of inhale and exhale.<br>
· Whenever someone feels breathless, dizzy or anxious: back to natural breathing, never force the count.<br>
· Count slowly, leave space between the numbers so everyone can follow their own breath.<br>
· To finish, invite everyone to let go of the numbers and observe 2–3 natural breaths — what has changed since the beginning?
</div>
